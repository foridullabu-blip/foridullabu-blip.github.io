Research

From policy on paper to a learner in the room

A policy changes a child's education only after it passes through institutions and classrooms. My research follows that path and asks where marginalised learners get lost along it.

  1. Level 1

    Policy

    National education and disability-inclusion policy; what it promises and to whom.

  2. Level 2

    Institutions

    Universities, schools and NGOs that interpret policy, with their own workloads and cultures.

  3. Level 3

    Classrooms

    Teachers adapting instruction, support staff, and the practice of inclusion day to day.

  4. Level 4

    Learners

    Children with disabilities, working children, and others at the margins of access.

Inclusive education

How do disability-responsive policies reach, or miss, children with additional needs?

Implementation

How do institutional practices in Bangladesh shape what policy becomes in practice?

Inequality & access

What drives exclusion for marginalised learners, from urban slums to under-resourced schools?
