Research statement

I study how disabled children take part in decisions about their own schooling in Bangladesh. Enrolment has grown, but my fieldwork with Save the Children showed that being in school does not give children a voice there.

Drawing on Lundy's model of participation, I am interested in everyday school decisions as children, parents and teachers experience them, and in how national inclusion policy reaches the classroom. I am completing a systematic review on this topic and am seeking a PhD place for 2027.
